Privacy policy

Jaxon Personal System is a personal productivity and automation application operated by Jaxon Dechant for the owner's use. Contact: jaxondechant34@gmail.com.

Google information and its purpose

The current Google authorization includes Gmail read, send and modify permissions; Calendar access; full Drive access; read-only Contacts; and Sheets and Docs access. Depending on the requested or scheduled task, the application can access Google email content and metadata, calendar events, Drive files, documents, spreadsheets, and contact information. It uses relevant information to perform personal-assistant functions such as organizing information, summarizing content, drafting material, and carrying out authorized tasks. Permissions describe capabilities; not every task accesses every category.

OAuth access and refresh tokens authorize Google API requests without giving the application the owner's Google password. Tokens are stored on the owner's computer and are not published with this website.

Processing and service providers

The assistant runs on the owner's Windows computer. Relevant task content, including Google information when necessary for the task, may be sent to OpenAI through the configured direct Codex connection. The application is not entirely local-only. The owner has disabled the ChatGPT account's model-improvement setting for this connection. Provider retention and processing remain subject to applicable provider policies.

Task messages and outputs may be sent through Discord or Telegram, depending on the configured destination. These services process and retain information under their own policies. Google holds information stored in Google services. The owner should use private destinations for personal information; information sent into a shared chat may be visible to its participants.

OmniRoute is not part of the application's active AI-processing route. The main assistant and configured helper tasks use the direct OpenAI connection, with no configured alternative-provider fallback. The Gemini API credential has been disabled in this application. These controls describe the current configuration; changes to providers or processing practices require a new privacy review and an updated policy.

Use restrictions

Google user data is used to provide the application's requested personal-assistant functions. The operator does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train generalized AI models. External processing is described above; the operator's own use restrictions do not establish that every available provider follows the same restrictions. The Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements, governs permitted use and transfer of Google API information. This policy is a description of the application, not a certification that all available processing routes have been audited for compliance.

Storage and retention

Authorized content and generated outputs may remain in Google services, local files, assistant conversation history, task records, logs, messaging services, and backups. Owner-controlled records remain until the owner deletes them; no automatic expiry or guaranteed deletion deadline is promised. This policy does not promise a particular unverified encryption standard.

Deletion and revocation

Contact jaxondechant34@gmail.com with privacy or deletion requests. Deletion must consider local files, assistant history, logs, messaging copies, and backups separately. The operator reviews requests and deletes owner-controlled copies through available controls. Provider-held copies, legal retention, and third-party deletion are subject to those services' policies and available controls.

The owner can revoke Google authorization through Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorized access with that grant; it does not automatically erase information already stored or sent to another service.
