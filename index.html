Jaxon Personal System

A private, owner-operated personal productivity assistant for organizing documents, spreadsheets, email, and calendar tasks. It is intended for its owner, not public registration.

Google connection

The assistant uses Google OAuth to access services authorized by its owner. Its currently configured permissions cover Gmail reading, sending and modification; Calendar access; Drive access; read-only Contacts; and Docs and Sheets access. A granted permission does not mean every feature is used on every task.

Google account passwords are not collected by this information website. This website contains no sign-in form and does not operate the assistant.

Data handling

The assistant runs on the owner’s computer, but relevant task content may also be processed by configured external AI providers and delivered through messaging services. Records remain until the owner deletes them; no automatic deletion deadline is promised. Revoking Google access does not erase previously stored copies.
